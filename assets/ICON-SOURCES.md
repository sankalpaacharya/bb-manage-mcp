# Harness marks

Claude, Gemini, OpenCode, and Cursor SVG paths come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) (CC0). The Codex/OpenAI mark matches BB's bundled `provider-codex/icons/codex.svg`. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners. All paths are bundled locally, rendered in the current BB text color, and paired with harness labels.
